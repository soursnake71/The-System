Sync & notes

Note map

POWER: slide the ON-OFF switch (top right) to switch the machine on.

SHIFT works like the original: hold it and press a key for the function printed above the key. It also latches: tap SHIFT on its own and the next key gets its shift function (SHIFT shows on the display); tap SHIFT again to cancel.

Play a pattern: A|B|C|D picks the bank (A, B presets, C, D yours), 1-8 the rhythm, START / STOP. While it runs a new choice waits for the end of the bar.

Write: STEP (SHIFT+4): the cursor blinks on the display. The voice keys put a note in or take it out at the cursor, ACCENT the accent; START moves on a step, STOP goes back. You can also tap the display grid.

Write: TAP (SHIFT+5): START, then play the voice keys in time (hold ACCENT for accents); notes land on the nearest step.

12/16 (SHIFT+6): 12 steps (triplets) or 16. PATTERN CLEAR (SHIFT+7, twice) empties the pattern.

Write: SONG (SHIFT+3): pick bank and rhythm, then ENTER (SHIFT+O.HIHAT) puts it at this measure and moves on. MEASURE DOWN / UP (SHIFT+BASS / SNARE) move, MEASURE RESET (SHIFT+8) goes to 1, D.C. (SHIFT+C.HIHAT) ends the song here and repeats it (press again for no repeat). SHIFT+I·II picks song I or II.

Play: SONG (SHIFT+1) and START. Play: PATTERN is SHIFT+2.

KNUCKLE: SHIFT+HAND CLAP plays (and writes) the knuckle, a voice the original doesn't have; it shows in the BASS DRUM row as an open square. On the display the cymbal shows in the O.HIHAT row and the hand clap in the SNARE DRUM row the same way.

Knobs: drag up / down (double-tap for the default). TEMPO shows the BPM on the display while you turn it.
