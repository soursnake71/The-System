ASR-10 ROM Recorder

Records the boot ROM that the explorer test disk sends on MIDI Out.

  1. Close Ableton and MIDI-OX (only one program can use a MIDI port).
  2. Click Connect and allow MIDI access (including "system exclusive") when Chrome asks.
  3. Pick the input your ASR-10 MIDI Out is plugged into. Choose All inputs if unsure.
  4. Boot the ASR-10 with the test disk. Wait until the bar is full (about 3-4 minutes).
  5. Click Save and upload the .syx file.
