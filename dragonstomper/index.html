Starpath's 1982 Supercharger adventure, reverse engineered from the original tapes and rebuilt in 3D. Same monsters, same dice, same three parts.

Original game by Steve Landrum, Starpath Corporation, 1982. Fan remake.

How to play

Three parts, as on the original tapes: cross the Enchanted Countryside and get past the bridge guard, gear up in the Village, then go down into the Dragon's Cave and kill the dragon.

Controls

  • Move: stick (left half of the screen on a phone), WASD or arrows, or a gamepad.
  • FIRE (Space / Enter) opens the menu: ▲ MOVE ▶ FIGHT ◀ USE ▼ STATUS. Push the stick that way (or 1-4) to pick.
  • BACK (Esc / X) closes menus. In the dragon's lair FIRE attacks and BACK opens the menu.

Searching and fighting

  • Walk into trees, huts, temples, rocks, lakes and swamps to search them. You might find gold or an item - or a monster, a trap or a pit.
  • Fights take turns. FIGHT swings, RUN tries to get away, USE uses an item. STR is your life; DEX decides who hits. STR slowly comes back while you walk.
  • Churches: DONATE 200 fills your STR, DONATE 15 might give one point.

Items

  • CROSS, RING, STAFF, CHARM, POTION are magic. Each game the five effects are dealt out at random: full strength (and +2 max), better DEX, worse DEX, poison, or protection. The only clue the game gives is "THE RING FEELS WEIRD" - watch your numbers.
  • KEY opens locked doors and a CHEST (75 gold and maybe an item). HANDAXE hits harder, SHIELD blocks a point of damage.
  • The bridge guard wants your ID - the PAPER. Maniacs carry it. Or pay him, or fight him if you are very brave.
  • In the village: the Trade Shop sells a LANTERN (light in the cave), MEDICINE (cures fever), ROPE (pits), CHAIN armour, ELIXIR, VITAMINS, a LONGBOW (hit the dragon from afar), a SAPPHIRE (stops dragon magic cancelling your spells) and a RUBY (halves dragon fire). The Magic Shop sells BLAST, FLASH, STUN, UNLOCK, VISION and PROTECT. Warriors will fight for you, for a price.

About

The monsters, items, scenery shapes, colours, font, text and combat rules were pulled straight out of the 6502 code on the three Supercharger loads. See REVERSE_ENGINEERING.md in the project for the details.
