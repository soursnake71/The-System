          Omega Twelve

          Pads play the sample at the pitch set for each pad: Bank A is 0 to +7 semitones, B −8 to −1, C +8 to +15, D −16 to −9. Set any pad's pitch on the Multi Mode fader page.

          Faders control the page lit beside them. Performance steps through the pages; tap a page name to go straight to it. Double-tap a fader to reset it.

          Display: tap the waveform to put the cursor there, drag to move it. Drag the IN and OUT flags, or press Set In / Set Out to put them at the cursor. Nothing is selected for you.

          Set-up, Disk, Sync, Sample, Tempo open their menus on the display: tap an item or press its number. Choices change with ◀ ▶; numbers are typed on the keypad and confirmed with Yes.

          With no menu open, ◀ ▶ change preset, the keypad types a preset number (Yes loads it) and Enter opens the preset list.

          Programming: the keys do the Edit functions, or the Master functions when the Master light is on.

          Run/Stop plays the selection (from the cursor if it's inside), Tap/Repeat loops it, Stop All stops everything.

          Everything is played live: turn anything and you hear it at once. The SP-1200 and SP-12 run their circuits (anti-alias filter, outputs 1-8, the SSM2044 and its envelope) from the service-manual parts.
