Put recordings of **your own** voice here (mp3, wav or m4a), 1-3 minutes total.
These files are ignored by git, so they stay off GitHub.

Tips for a good clone:
- A quiet room, phone held about 6 inches away, no music or TV.
- Talk the way you talk to them on the phone: relaxed, warm, everyday phrases
  ("Hey, how are you doing?", "Oh, that sounds nice", "I love you, talk soon").
- One person only. No long silences.
