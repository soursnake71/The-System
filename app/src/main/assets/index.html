16 Levels plays the selected pad on all 16 pads, stepping one setting across them: Tune in semitones (pad 9 is the original pitch), Velocity, Decay, Filter, or Slices (the sound cut into 16 parts). Note Repeat retriggers a held pad in time with the tempo.

SP-1200 mode resamples every pad to 26.04 kHz at 12 bits with no anti-aliasing filter, pitches by skipping and repeating samples, and holds each output sample with no smoothing. Each of the 8 channels plays one sound at a time. Channels 1–2 run through a modeled SSM2044 ladder filter, 3–6 through fixed low-pass filters, and 7–8 go out raw. MPC60 mode instead converts every pad to 40 kHz at 12 bits through an anti-aliasing filter; W-30 mode to 30 kHz at 12 bits.

Tap a pad to play it and edit it here. Load several fills the pads from many files at once, matching names like kick, snare and hat to their pads. Pads also play from MIDI channel 10.
