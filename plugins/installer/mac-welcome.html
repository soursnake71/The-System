SPQuencer1200

A 24 PPQ sequencer for the E-mu SP-1200 and the Rossum SP-1200, as a VST3 and Audio Unit plugin.

It installs into the standard plug-in folders:

  • VST3: /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
  • Audio Unit: /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components

Click Customize on the next pages to install only one of them.
