SPQuencer1200 is installed

Ableton Live: Settings > Plug-Ins > turn on Use VST3 Plug-In System Folders and click Rescan. SPQuencer1200 is under Plug-Ins > VST3 > The Omega System.

To send its notes to your SP-1200, put it on a MIDI track, then on a second MIDI track set MIDI From to that track / SPQuencer1200 and MIDI To your SP-1200's MIDI port, with monitoring set to In.

Logic Pro: it appears under AU Instruments > The Omega System.
