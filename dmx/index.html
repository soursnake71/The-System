Omega DMX

  • Voice keys play the sounds, in columns under their faders. Long-press one to rename it. With RECORD lit and the pattern playing they record where you play them (with ACCENT lit, accented).
  • EDIT shows the pattern's steps in place of the keys: tap a light for a hit, again to clear, drag to paint. Tap a name to hear and pick the voice.
  • Keypad: 1–9 and 0 pick the ten patterns of the kit (a dot marks the ones with hits); < > step through them. A new pattern starts when the playing one ends.
  • TEMPO: then type the tempo on the keypad, or < > for one step. SWING steps 50–71%.
  • SONG: then keypad numbers add patterns to the song, ERASE takes the last one off; SONG again to leave. With a song, PLAY runs it.
  • COPY: then a number copies this pattern there. ERASE clears the picked voice, CLEAR the whole pattern (press twice). FACTORY puts the factory pattern back. KIT picks the kit.
  • TUNE: then type 00–99 on the keypad, or < > for 5: the picked voice's pitch trimmer (50 is the centre). A voice key picks the voice. Higher is faster and shorter, as on the hardware.
  • CIRCUIT: DMX plays each sound the way a DMX voice card would: written into the card's ROM (8-bit AM6070 codes, at the card's own clock, cut to the ROM's size), clocked out by its 555, held between ticks, its decay envelope on the DAC's reference, its three Sallen-Key filters, and the DMX's stereo mix and audio-taper faders. CIRCUIT: CLEAN plays the sounds as recorded. The DMX kit starts clean, since it was recorded from a DMX.
  • Faders: Bass, Snare, Hi-Hat, Toms, Cymbal, Perc 1, Perc 2, the Metronome (all the way down: no click) and the master Volume.
  • MIDI: General MIDI drum notes play the kit (36 kick, 38 snare, 42 hat …); every voice also sits on its own note from C3 (60) up. MIDI clock and start / stop drive the sequencer.
  • EXPORT WAV saves the pattern (four times) or the song.
