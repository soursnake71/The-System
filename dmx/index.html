Omega DMX

  • Grid: tap a light to place a hit (with ACCENT lit, an accented hit), tap again to clear. Drag to paint. Tap a name to pick the voice; long-press it to rename.
  • Voice keys play the sounds. With RECORD lit and the pattern playing they record where you play them.
  • Keypad: 1–9 and 0 pick the ten patterns of the kit (a dot marks the ones with hits); < > step through them. A new pattern starts when the playing one ends.
  • TEMPO: then type the tempo on the keypad, or < > for one step. SWING steps 50–71%.
  • SONG: then keypad numbers add patterns to the song, ERASE takes the last one off; SONG again to leave. With a song, PLAY runs it.
  • COPY: then a number copies this pattern there. ERASE clears the picked voice, CLEAR the whole pattern (press twice). FACTORY puts the factory pattern back.
  • Faders set Bass, Snare, Hi-Hat, Toms, Cymbal, Perc 1, Perc 2 and the master Volume.
  • MIDI: General MIDI drum notes play the kit (36 kick, 38 snare, 42 hat …); every voice also sits on its own note from C3 (60) up. MIDI clock and start / stop drive the sequencer.
  • EXPORT WAV saves the pattern (four times) or the song.
