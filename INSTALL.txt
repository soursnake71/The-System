THE F.A.R.P. - VST3 for Windows

Install
  1. Copy the FARP.vst3 folder to  C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\
  2. In your music software, rescan plugins. It shows up as an instrument called "The F.A.R.P."
  "FARP Standalone.exe" runs it on its own (pick your audio device and MIDI keyboard in its Options).

Needs
  Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit). The plugin window uses Microsoft Edge WebView2, which Windows already has.

ROMs
  Open the plugin, go to INSTRUMENTS > ROMS and press "Change..." to pick the folder with your ROM zips
  (the same MAME-style zips the app uses). Nothing is copied: the plugin reads them from that folder.
  The sounds you pick are saved with your project.

Playing
  MIDI from your DAW track plays whatever the on-screen keyboard plays: the instrument picked on the
  INSTRUMENTS screen, or the split. Mod wheel, pitch bend and the sustain pedal work.

Vocoder
  The vocoder's voice can be the speech lines (VOICE tab) or audio sent into the plugin: route a mic or
  an audio track into the plugin's input (or sidechain), and choose "Plugin input" under SPEECH > Voice from.
  Chords from your DAW speak the lines the same way the on-screen keyboard does.
